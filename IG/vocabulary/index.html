    Vocabulary for IGCSE biology

    Cell Biology

    Word 中文翻译 Definition
    cell 细胞 The basic unit of life in all living organisms.
    organelle 细胞器 A small structure inside a cell with a specific function.
    nucleus 细胞核 The control center of a cell that contains DNA.
    chromosome 染色体 A structure made of DNA that carries genetic information.
    cytoplasm 细胞质 The jelly-like fluid inside the cell that holds organelles.
    membrane 膜 A thin layer that surrounds a cell and controls what enters and leaves.
    mitochondria 线粒体 The powerhouse of the cell, where energy is produced.
    ribosome 核糖体 A small organelle where proteins are made.
    synthesis 合成 The process of making complex substances from simpler ones.
    endoplasmic reticulum 内质网 A network of membranes in a cell that helps move and produce materials.
    chloroplast 叶绿体 The organelle in plant cells where photosynthesis happens.
    vacuole 液泡 A storage space in cells, especially large in plant cells.
    permanent 永久的 Describes cells that do not divide again after maturing.
    ciliated 有纤毛的 Having small hair-like structures that help move substances.
    guard (cell) 保卫细胞 A cell in plant leaves that controls the opening and closing of stomata.
    xylem 木质部 A tissue that carries water from roots to other parts of the plant.
    nerve (cell) 神经细胞 A cell that carries electrical signals in the body.
    haemoglobin 血红蛋白 A protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen.
    cone (cell) 锥体细胞 A type of eye cell that detects color.
    optic 视神经的 Related to the eye or vision.
    palisade 栅栏细胞 A type of leaf cell with many chloroplasts, found near the leaf surface.
    mesophyll 叶肉组织 The inner tissue of a leaf, where photosynthesis takes place.
    sperm 精子 The male sex cell used in reproduction.
    egg 卵子 The female sex cell used in reproduction.
    tissue 组织 A group of similar cells that work together to do a job.
    organ 器官 A part of the body made of different tissues working together.
    system 系统 A group of organs working together for a function.
    spongy (mesophyll) 海绵状（叶肉）组织 Leaf tissue with air spaces for gas exchange.
    diffusion 扩散 The movement of particles from high to low concentration.
    concentration 浓度 The amount of substance in a certain volume.
    gradient 梯度 A difference in concentration across a space.
    passive (transport) 被动（运输） Movement that does not require energy.
    osmosis 渗透作用 The movement of water across a membrane from high to low water potential.
    potential (water) 水势 The tendency of water to move from one area to another.
    hypotonic 低渗 A solution with lower solute concentration than the cell.
    hypertonic 高渗 A solution with higher solute concentration than the cell.
    isotonic 等渗 A solution with the same solute concentration as the cell.
    turgidity 膨胀性 The firmness of a plant cell due to water intake.
    turgor (pressure) 膨压 The pressure inside a plant cell from water pushing against the cell wall.
    wilt 枯萎 When a plant droops due to loss of water and turgor.
    plasmolysis 质壁分离 When the cell membrane pulls away from the cell wall due to water loss.
    flaccid 松弛的 A plant cell that has lost water and is soft.
    carrier (protein) 载体蛋白 A protein in a membrane that helps move substances across it.

    Classification of organisms

    Word 中文翻译 Definition
    nutrition 营养 The process of taking in and using food for energy and growth.
    respiration 呼吸作用 The chemical process that releases energy from food.
    movement 运动 The ability of an organism to change position or move parts of its body.
    growth 生长 The increase in size or number of cells in an organism.
    excretion 排泄 The removal of waste products made by cells.
    sensitivity 对刺激的反应 The ability to detect and respond to changes in the environment.
    reproduction 繁殖 The process of making more of the same kind of organism.
    binomial 双名法 A naming system using two names: genus and species.
    prokaryote 原核生物 A simple cell without a nucleus (e.g. bacteria).
    bacteria 细菌 Single-celled prokaryotic organisms.
    plasmid 质粒 A small circular piece of DNA in bacteria.
    eukaryote 真核生物 Organisms with cells that have a nucleus.
    kingdom 界（生物分类） The highest group in biological classification (e.g. animal, plant).
    protoctist 原生生物 Mostly single-celled eukaryotes, not animals, plants, or fungi.
    fungi 真菌 Organisms like mushrooms and molds that feed by absorbing nutrients.
    host 宿主 An organism that supports a parasite or virus.
    fish 鱼类 Cold-blooded animals that live in water and breathe through gills.
    amphibian 两栖动物 Cold-blooded animals that live both in water and on land.
    reptile 爬行动物 Cold-blooded animals with dry, scaly skin.
    bird 鸟类 Warm-blooded animals with feathers, wings, and beaks.
    mammal 哺乳动物 Warm-blooded animals with hair or fur that produce milk for young.
    arthropod 节肢动物 Invertebrates with jointed legs and segmented bodies.
    crustacean 甲壳类动物 Arthropods with hard shells, like crabs and shrimp.
    myriapod 多足类动物 Arthropods with many body segments and legs, like centipedes.
    insect 昆虫 Arthropods with three body parts and six legs.
    arachnid 蜘蛛纲动物 Arthropods with eight legs, like spiders and scorpions.
    fern 蕨类植物 Non-flowering plants that reproduce with spores.
    shoot 茎部/芽 The above-ground part of a plant, including stems and leaves.
    root 根 The part of a plant that anchors it and absorbs water and nutrients.
    dicotyledon 双子叶植物 Plants with two seed leaves.
    monocotyledon 单子叶植物 Plants with one seed leaf.
    dichotomous 二分法 A classification key that splits groups into two choices at each step.
